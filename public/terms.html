What this site is

Rate Your Judge Colorado is an independent public project. It lets people share their own experience of a judge or prosecutor in a Colorado courtroom. It is not part of the State of Colorado, its courts or any commission, and it gives no legal advice.

What you agree to when you rate

  • You were really there and you are describing your own experience.
  • You write facts and behavior you saw, not insults, threats, rumors or guesses about why someone did something.
  • You do not post private details: names of minors, victims, case numbers, addresses, or anything sealed or confidential.
  • You rate a person once per hearing and do not try to rig results with extra phones or help from others.
  • You are 18 or older.
  • You are responsible for what you write. Your comment is your own statement, not a statement by this project.

What we do with comments

  • Comments appear right away, marked “Not reviewed yet”, and are then approved or removed by a moderator. Moderators may refuse, shorten or remove a comment.
  • Scores and comments are opinions of the people who wrote them, not statements by this project.
  • We may remove anything that breaks these rules, and may block abuse of the site.

Limits

The site is a pilot and is provided as it is, without promises that it is always available or that every score is accurate. The people listed are real public officials; ratings and comments are the opinions of the people who wrote them. During the pilot we collect feedback to improve the service. Do not use it to decide a legal matter.

Questions and corrections

If something about you or a ruling is wrong, see Corrections and removal. Contact: hello@rateyourjudgecolorado.com.
